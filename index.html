The problem

Discovery usually starts blank

SOPs and interviews alone can miss what happens between apps: repeated steps, manual handoffs and exceptions.

  1. SOPsThey describe intended work, not actual work.
  2. InterviewsOn their own, they depend on recall and leave out edge cases.

How it works today

Evidence → Map → Action

With permission, we capture scoped desktop activity. We reconstruct the real sequence and propose changes for people to review.

  1. 1 · CaptureScoped desktop activity, collected with permission.
  2. 2 · ReconstructCross-app sequences, repeated steps, manual handoffs and exceptions.
  3. 3 · GenerateProcess maps, documentation, and automation candidates and blueprints.
  4. 4 · ValidatePeople confirm the findings before anyone commits to automation. We map processes, not rate people.
